Introduction to Artificial Intelligence in the Workplace


Artificial intelligence is changing the way we work. AI tools can help employees 
complete tasks faster, make better decisions, and focus on more creative work. 
However, many employees feel uncertain about how AI will affect their jobs.


This module will help you understand what AI is, how it works in simple terms, 
and how you can use it effectively in your daily work. By the end of this module, 
you will be able to identify AI tools relevant to your role, use basic AI features 
confidently, and understand the limitations of AI so you can verify its outputs.


It is important to remember that AI is a tool, not a replacement for human 
judgement. The best results come from combining AI capabilities with your own 
expertise and critical thinking.
