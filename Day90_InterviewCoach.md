\# Day 90 — AI Interview Answer Coach

\#\# What I Built  
An AI interview coach that takes any interview question and a draft answer,  
scores it across 4 dimensions (Clarity, Relevance, Confidence, Structure),  
identifies weak phrases, rewrites the answer using STAR method, gives  
power phrases to memorise, and predicts the follow-up question.  
Pre-loaded with 32 real questions across 4 role categories.

\#\# What I Learned  
\- 90 days of consistent effort compounds into something real  
\- Building tools you actually need is the best motivation to keep going  
\- Every concept I learned — RAG, JSON, APIs, functions, Streamlit —  
  came together across these final projects  
\- The gap between "learning AI" and "building with AI" closed completely

\#\# How I'd Explain This in an Interview  
"My final project was an AI interview coach — which I'm using right now  
to prepare for this conversation. It scores my answers, identifies weak  
phrases, rewrites them using the STAR method, and predicts follow-up  
questions. I built it because preparation is a skill, and I wanted to  
engineer my own preparation system."
