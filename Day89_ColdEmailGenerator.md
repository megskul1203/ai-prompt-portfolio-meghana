\# Day 89 — AI Cold Email Generator

\#\# What I Built  
An AI tool that generates personalised cold emails to hiring managers.  
Takes company name, role, and context — outputs a subject line, email body,  
why it works, best send time, follow-up tip, and a LinkedIn connection note.  
Three tone options: Professional, Conversational, and Ultra-short.

\#\# What I Learned  
\- Cold emails bypass ATS systems entirely — direct human contact  
\- Personalisation is the difference between deleted and replied to  
\- The same AI prompt engineering skills that power my apps also power  
  my job search — the tools I built are now working for me  
\- A portfolio link in an email is more powerful than any resume line

\#\# How I'd Explain This in an Interview  
"I built a cold email generator that writes personalised outreach emails  
by referencing specific projects from my portfolio that are relevant to  
each company. I built it because I needed it — and using it is how I  
reached out to you for this interview."
