\# Day 86 — Resume Rebuild

\#\# What I Did  
Rebuilt my resume from scratch to reflect who I actually am now —  
an AI-powered L\&D professional with 9 live deployed applications —  
rather than a content writer. Changed the title, summary, skills  
section, and added the full AI portfolio.

\#\# What I Learned  
\- A resume is a positioning document, not a history document  
\- The title and first line decide whether a recruiter reads further  
\- AI skills should appear before L\&D tools for AI-targeted roles  
\- One page is the standard for under 3 years of experience  
\- Showing a promotion (Writer to Developer) builds credibility

\#\# How I'd Explain This in an Interview  
"I rebuilt my resume to lead with my AI portfolio — 9 live deployed  
applications — because that is my strongest differentiator. My L\&D  
background is the context, but my AI projects are the proof."
